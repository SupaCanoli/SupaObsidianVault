Made to be able to show differences between the [[Strongarm Supa's Universe]] and [[Solarpunk Universe Common Name Here]] by [[Supa Kosmas|Dr. Kosmas]] before using the [[Full Quantum Thrash Allocation]] to change universes. There are two forms to the naming convention, which include a Common Name version for non-specific needs, such as referencing to his original universe as [[Strongarm Supa's Universe| Strongarm Supa's Universe]], or his new universe as the [[Solarpunk Universe Common Name Here|Solarpunk Supa's Universe]], etc. For more specific needs, there is a more in-depth format for the universal naming system, based on an outdated format that was used in networking before he made it out of his Masters of Science in Computer Engineering in college. Each universe is represented with different varying levels of hexadecimal formatting, depending on what level of specificity you need from a set of universes.
