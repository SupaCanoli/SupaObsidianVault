A corporation that has existed since 2038, starting out in the creation of cybernetics for medical purposes. They eventually gain enough money to start branching out into other types of work. However, the company became focused more for profits, which meant they began to branch out into other, possibly more violent branches of innovation and creation. The company did not want to have to find new scientists for their new endeavors, and as such, decided to change the employment contracts for scientists that makes the rights for any innovations they create automatically become ownership of the company's to do as they please as soon as a report has been given about the invention in question. They would then use these with a new, smaller set of scientists, who's purpose is to reverse-engineer the cybernetics made for medical use, and find ways to use them for warfare purposes.
