The genetic makeup of people who undergo the process of B-10 Symbiosis becomes quite unique from person to person, however there are some specific attributes that would be similar, if not entirely the same across every subject in question. All original cells of the subject in question can still serve the same purposes that the human body require, however, they now hold an extra technological attribute that make them able to diffuse or permeate throughout the body and reallocate themselves.
