Developed by [[Supa Kosmas|Dr. Kosmas]], this invention was made as a method of escaping [[Strongarm Corporations|Strongarm Corp.]] after creating the [[B-10 Logical System]] as a whole by escaping this universe, and finding another suitable version that could hold him, while also ensuring that this type of corruption would not be an issue in the new area in question. This was made using the knowledge from other versions of himself through [[B-10 Quantum Memory Thrashing]] on the [[B-10 Logical System|B-10]]. After successfully building a method to use his own body to transport along with his proof of functionality, he managed to search through [[B-10 Quantum Memory Thrashing]] this was attempted to be reverse-engineered by the [[Project Hegemony]] within Strongarm Corp., however to not much avail before needing to start from scratch to catch with their own method of catching up in order to find out the information in which he had kept a secret from their universe.
