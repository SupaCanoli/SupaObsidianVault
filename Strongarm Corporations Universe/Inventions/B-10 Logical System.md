Developed by [[Supa Kosmas|Dr. Kosmas]], the creation of this system includes many different features and capabilities as essentially a symbiosis with the human body and nanotechnology. Some functions of this are things such as the [[B-10 Operating System]], [[B-10 Quantum Memory Thrashing]], and it's main function that was created unintentionally, the [[B-10 Cellular Structure System]]. This system was so advanced in it's design, that after [[Supa Kosmas|Dr. Kosmas]] had run the mutation process for the [[B-10 Logical System]] on himself and seen what this was capable of, he had realized that the true existence of [[Project Economize]] would take his invention to be able to hurt billions of people, being able to reform and shapeshift, while also essentially thinking at over 10x the speed of the average human if trained to work well enough alongside the symbiotic nanotechnology. This immediately prompted him to want to keep this invention a secret, and thus, his use of this invention to create the [[Full Quantum Thrash Allocation]] began work.
