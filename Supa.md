# History in the Strongarm Corporations Universe

Supa is a scientist for [[Strongarm Corporations|Strongarm Corp.]] with a PhD in biomedical engineering and a Master's Degree in computer engineering. Up until the last couple months of his time working in the company, he had not established his own personal project within the company that was fully his work. He had been questioning and asking his head manager, [[Arden]], for a while whether he could be the first to work on nanotechnology because of how taboo it seemed to everyone at the time. He was eventually approved to work on his own in a new sector called [[Project Nano-Symbiosis]]. 

# [[Supa|Supa's]] Work In [[Project Nano-Symbiosis]] 
[[Supa]] is the sole inventor of the [[Universe Hop Mechanism]] and [[B10 Biological System|B 1/0 Biological System]].
