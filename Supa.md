# History

Supa is a PhD holding scientist for [[Strongarm Corporations|Strongarm Corp.]], who up until the last couple months of his time working in the company, had not established his own personal project within the company that was fully his work. He had been questioning and asking <insert higher up people's names here> for a while if he could be the first to work on nanotechnology because of how taboo it seemed to everyone at the time. He was eventually approved to work on his own in a new sector called [[Project Nano-Symbiosis]]. He is the sole inventor of the [[Universe Hop Mechanism]] and the [[B10 Biological System|B 1/0 Biological System]]
